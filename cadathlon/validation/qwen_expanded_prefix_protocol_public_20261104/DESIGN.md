# Proposed expanded-prefix Qwen calibration protocol

This is a distinct, public-only protocol proposal. No tokenizer, source prompt/token body, checkpoint, model, coefficient selector, certificate run or trajectory is executed by this artifact. The original three-candidate protocol, candidate3 bytes, its completed416/112/112 outcomes, and the failed fixed row0 experiment remain unchanged. Prior observed failure motivated this design; the design is therefore informed by that history, while the row0 value and all validation extrema are excluded from coefficient selection.

The calibration workload is exactly the same96 authored original prompts, now at every prefix length1 through32. Each original S32 prefix supplies its firstL tokens; a separately authorized tokenizer/input-binding step must verify exact correspondence to the original S8/S16 entries and decode seeds. This gives3072 prefills, plus the same64 teacher-forced and64 free-running steps, for3200 workload entries. The old288 prefills at lengths8/16/32 and128 decode entries form an explicit416-entry subset. These are entry counts, not unique-token-context claims: repeated short prefixes remain in the declared denominators. No prompt may be removed or replaced using activation ranges or output errors.

The observer retains all113 RMS inputs,28 centered-softmax sites and28 SiLU sites. All421 operator outputs,28 block outputs and151936 final logits are compared on each context; all propagated normalization, exp, reciprocal and rational-SiLU intermediate domains remain mandatory. Teacher comparison uses the original native preceding tokens. Free-running comparison uses the evaluated circuit's actual prefix, with native/exact capture on the same prefix. Stopped suffixes remain explicitly unmeasured and failed in the unchanged complete-workload denominator.

Selection is a normalization-only successor. Every one of113 sites uses the same rule on the expanded native-float32/exact-float64 input-range union: first[epsilon-clamped0.8minimum,1.2maximum], then a factor4 expansion about positive scale with the same epsilon floor. Candidate2/candidate3 validation extrema and the special checkpoint-row0 value are not selection inputs. No per-site exception is permitted. The fixed balanced-Chebyshev ladder is16/32/64/128/256/512, with the exact stored coefficient/rounded affine-map residual certificate at1e-6 relative error and the unchanged2049 linear/geometric binary64 diagnostic. Failure falls back to a newly computed exact-rounded-first-affine static-Newton certificate/count (maximum64) on that site's new domain. Counts from old endpoints cannot be reused as if certified on new endpoints.

All28 SiLU and28 softmax rules, exp8 and the K-specific reciprocal contracts stay byte-identical to candidate3. The larger workload observes their domains and the new norm certificates must still imply the protected public SiLU radii. A protected-rule violation makes this proposal unsupported; it does not silently broaden another domain or change counts. At most3 new-protocol candidates may use the same whole-site rule after calibration-only failure: the next range union may include observed valid-prefix/rejected-input calibration values, but cannot evaluate an already rejected operator, discard failures, or fit coefficients to output errors. Every manifest and attempt remains preserved. The original protocol's maximum3 is not retroactively extended.

The original numeric gates remain unchanged: exact-reference/native1e-3 maxabs and1e-5 nRMSE with exact top1; approximate operator/block nRMSE0.05; full-logit nRMSE0.1 with denominator floor0.01; complete domain coverage. The same0.9 aggregate rates imply2765/3072 prefill matches and58/64 teacher matches; calibration free-running requires12/16 complete sequences. Report every prefix length separately without introducing a different per-length acceptance threshold. HE-versus-approximate0.01 nRMSE/0.10 maxabs remain wholly separate, untested gates.

A new authored32-prompt validation design is frozen in `inputs/untouched_validation_design.json`: all prefixes1..32 give1024 prefill entries, plus eight fixed16-token seeds and four teacher/free steps give1088 total entries. The corresponding unchanged rates require922/1024 prefill,29/32 teacher and6/8 complete free sequences. No validation tokenization, disjointness or model measurement has occurred. Exact token bodies/hashes and their disjointness must be frozen BEFORE expanded calibration results or coefficient selection.

That disjointness gate compares each complete new validation token sequence against the EXPANDED3072 calibration prefixes, original teacher/free contexts, all older calibration/validation/fresh contexts, and the explicit row0 tokenID0 S1 regression. Equal short contexts count as overlap even if full texts or prompts differ. Report within-calibration and within-validation entries/unique sequences and cross-set intersections by length. Keep all entries/denominators, without deduplication. If tokenization reveals overlap or insufficient length, preserve this design and freeze a separately reviewed design revision before any new model run; do not silently replace an inconvenient prompt. The public synthetic accounting helper demonstrates this policy but reads no real token bodies.

The existing checkpoint-embedding row0 S1 is a separate post-freeze regression. Its identity is tokenID0 at original position0; generic first-S8 calibration provenance does not establish that identity. It is not a new selection input. Only the first fully calibration-passing, complete frozen candidate may open the new validation or row0 regression. Their failures cannot drive another candidate under this protocol; any later design needs another distinct protocol and untouched validation.

The new3072 prefills are about7.69 times the original416 entry count when combined with decode, but computation is not linear in entry count. Up to678 polynomial attempts per candidate may be needed. Existing93 C25 reserve failures, inverse-only refresh schedules, large normalization bounds and12931S32 continuation-refresh count do not transfer to a newly selected manifest without rederivation. Public certificates remain conditional exact-real bounds; sampled binary64 checks do not establish uniform floating/CKKS/refresh accuracy. Nothing here is an encrypted-readiness or practical-performance claim.
