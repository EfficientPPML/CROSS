For RMSNorm of width D and positive epsilon, each normalized coordinate has magnitude at most sqrt(D), hence the learned output is bounded by sqrt(D)*abs(gamma_i). For centered LayerNorm, the other D−1 centered coordinates sum to the negative of coordinate i. Cauchy therefore improves its bound to sqrt(D−1)*abs(gamma_i)+abs(beta_i). Both derivations are independent of calibration inputs. The script uses actual tensor width, exact F32/BF16-to-F64 conversion, rational verification of an outward square-root endpoint, and rationally checked outward rounding of the final bound.
